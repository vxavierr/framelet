# Framelet

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

Updated 2026-10-02, BRT.

## Platform
linux

## Users
Omarchy users who capture a detail, explain it visually and copy or save it quickly.

## Product Purpose
A small atelier for screenshots: select, annotate, compose and finish within the native capture overlay.

## Operating Context
Linux/Wayland, Hyprland, Omarchy and Quickshell. Qt 6/C++ with LayerShellQt. Super+C copies and Super+S saves during capture; Hyprland forwarding is documented explicitly.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Region/window/display selection, scrolling capture, annotations, image composition, code cards, OCR, finishes and PNG/JPEG export. Scrolling needs responsive, alignable content. Images are processed locally.

## Brand Commitments
Framelet is the product name. The atelier direction lives in the brush, saved five-colour palettes and optional materials. Sketchbook, Ink and Gallery are the named finishes. The capture flow remains immediate.

## Product Principles
- Capture and keyboard delivery stay immediate.
- Expression belongs to the image; controls remain clear and quiet.
- Preview and export share the same renderer.
- Existing preferences and drafts survive migration without overwriting newer files.

## Evidence on Hand
Native region and scrolling fixtures, QtTest suites, preview/export comparison, local source-package build and real exported finish examples. A marketplace listing requires its own submission and maintainer approval.
