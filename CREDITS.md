Framelet is based on Omaframe 0.7.4 by btsouth, MIT, commit 133da85819195573a597760d433bdcee85e4e824. Capture, scrolling, recording, OCR and the original native annotation tools are inherited and extended. Framing, image composition and code cards are native implementations inspired by Postcard by tahayvr (MIT), without opening that app. The Framelet brush, palettes and finishes are additions in this fork. Upstream and Omasnap license notices are preserved. Logo artwork is original; study references are not distributed as Framelet assets.
